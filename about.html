About Job Applications Tracker

Job Applications Tracker is a simple web application designed to help job seekers manage and organize their applications in one convenient place.

Keeping track of companies, job positions, interview dates, deadlines, and application progress can be challenging. This application makes the process easier by keeping everything organized.

Features

  • ✔ Add new job applications
  • ✔ Track application status
  • ✔ Save company details and notes
  • ✔ Monitor interviews and deadlines
  • ✔ View applications in one place

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple, attractive, and user-friendly tool that helps students and professionals stay organized during their job search journey.
